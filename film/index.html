The roast

We roast before dawn.

Small batches at four in the morning, while the city is still dark.

Cooling

Two minutes in the quiet.

The tray turns, the beans settle. This is where the sweetness locks in.

The grind

Ground to order.

Never in advance. The burrs only turn once your order is in — aroma does not wait.

First light

Twenty grams, one cup.

Weighed for every cup, into a rinsed paper filter, as the first light reaches the bar.

The bloom

Thirty seconds of patience.

The first water wakes the grounds. They rise, breathe out, and only then do we pour the rest.

Morning

Slow, by hand.

Four minutes, one steady pour. The light is up by the time it is ready.

The pour

Poured the moment you ask.

Straight from the server into your cup — still steaming.
